Gaps and fine print

Bills that are missing

  • Gas, May 2026. The meter readings on the April and June bills show 2.6 m³ in between, so about €3. That month is filled in from the meter.
  • Gas before April 2025. The meter already stood at 4,136 m³ then. Nothing from the 2024/25 winter is in the data.
  • Electricity before 9 March 2025. The first bill starts mid-month, so March 2025 is left out of the charts.

What the estimate assumes

  • Weather was not corrected for. A warmer or colder month than its twin a year earlier shifts the saving.
  • Everything electric other than heating is assumed to be used about as much as before the switch.
  • The heat pump was installed at no cost to you, so no payback period is counted. Servicing costs are not in the bills either.
  • An air-to-water heat pump loses efficiency as it gets colder outside, so the measured in mild months will likely be lower in January.
