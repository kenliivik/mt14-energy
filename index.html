Findings

  • Gas fell from 102 m³ in March 2026 to 25 m³ in April and about 2.5 m³ a month since.
  • April to September 2026 cost 628 €, against 689 € for the same months of 2025. Pricing 2025 usage at 2026 rates gives 767 € for staying on gas, so the switch saved about 139 € (Apr 14, May 39, Jun 18, Jul 7, Aug 23, Sep 38). Not weather-corrected.
  • Electricity rose by 849 kWh over those months while 2,775 kWh of gas disappeared: a ratio of 3.3.
  • The last gas-heated winter (Oct 2025 to Mar 2026) used 784 m³ (8,391 kWh) and cost 728 € in gas.
  • The heat pump came with the home at no cost, so there is no payback period: every saving is pure gain.
  • Next winter at October 2026 prices (gas about 1.43 €/m³ all-in, electricity about 19.5 c/kWh): gas would cost about 1,100 €, the heat pump about 640 € if 1 kWh of electricity replaces 2.5 kWh of gas. The pump wins whenever that ratio stays above about 1.5.
  • Still to measure: a full winter on the heat pump.
